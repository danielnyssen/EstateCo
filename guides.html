The biggest mistake is starting without a plan.

Most property clearances become stressful when family members start removing items before documents, valuables, keys, access, photos and sale goals are agreed. A simple plan protects time, value and relationships.

  • Decide what must be found before anything leaves the home.
  • Photograph each room before sorting starts.
  • Separate sentimental items from general contents.
  • Work out whether the home needs clearing, deep cleaning, gardening or sale preparation.

A simple starting order

  1. Secure the property and confirm access.
  2. Search for documents, keys, photos and valuables.
  3. Agree what family members want kept.
  4. Get a quote from photos or a site visit.
  5. Clear, clean and prepare the property for its next step.
