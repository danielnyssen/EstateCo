Estate clearance resources for families, executors and property owners

These guides are designed to become a practical resource library — not just short service pages. Each article answers a real question families ask when they need to clear, clean or prepare a property after a death, move, downsizing decision or difficult tenancy.

Start with the executor checklist if you are managing a deceased estate, or choose the guide that best matches your situation.

Every guide is written in plain English and structured like a helpful blog article, with checklists, decision points and practical next steps. This page can keep growing as Estate Co. publishes more articles over time.

Executor estate clearance checklist

A step-by-step guide for executors managing a property after a death, including what to secure, what to keep, how to communicate with beneficiaries and when to arrange clearance.

Deceased estate cleanout costs

A transparent guide to what affects estate cleanout pricing, from property size and access to disposal, deep cleaning, urgency, valuables and sale preparation.

Preparing a property for sale after a death

How to move from a lived-in family home to a property that is clear, clean, presentable and ready for agent appraisal, photography, inspections or settlement.

Remote estate clearance for interstate families

How to coordinate a property clearance when you cannot attend in person, including photo reviews, item approvals, access, progress updates and completion reporting.

Downsizing property clearance guide

A calm guide for families helping someone move into a smaller home, retirement village or aged care accommodation while managing belongings respectfully.

Hoarded property cleanout guide

A respectful, safety-first guide to clearing severely cluttered or long-neglected homes, including hidden valuables, hazards, staged sorting and deep cleaning.
