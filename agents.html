Free agent guide

Preparing a property after a deceased estate

A practical checklist agents can use when families need to clear, clean and prepare a home before sale.

1
Confirm access and decision makers

Identify executor, family contact, agent and any access restrictions before work starts.

2
Separate keep, donate, sell and dispose

This keeps the process respectful and avoids rushing important personal items.

3
Clear before photography

Presentation improves when bulky furniture, waste and clutter are removed first.

4
Finish with clean and garden tidy

A simple final reset can make the property feel calmer and more market-ready.
