Our Story

A Melbourne family business built to make a difficult process easier.

Estate Co. was created after seeing how difficult it can be to manage a home during times of transition.

Whether after the passing of a loved one, a move into aged care, or when a property has simply become overwhelming, clearing and preparing a home can feel stressful, time-consuming and emotionally heavy.

We believed there should be a better way — one that is organised, respectful and genuinely supportive. Our team helps families, executors, property owners and real estate agents clear, clean and prepare homes with care, discretion and professionalism.

Our goal is simple — to help people when they need it most. Every home is handled with care, compassion and respect for the people and memories connected to it.
