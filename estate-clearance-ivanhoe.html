What we do

Our Ivanhoe clearance services

We handle every aspect of estate clearance and property preparation in Ivanhoe — from initial sort to final clean.

Estate Cleanouts

Full property clear-outs following a passing, handled with care and discretion. Families and executors don't need to manage the physical work — we take care of everything from sorting and removal through to final clean.

Downsizing & Aged Care Transitions

Whole-home sorting and clearance when moving to a smaller home or aged care facility. We help separate what to keep, donate, store or remove so the transition is calm, organised and manageable.

Pre-Sale Property Preparation

Clearing unwanted contents and preparing homes for real estate photography, inspection or settlement. The property is presented clean, open and ready for market — without owners needing to lift a finger.

Hoarded & Neglected Properties

Clearance of heavily cluttered or long-neglected homes requiring more than standard cleaning. Our team works discreetly and without judgement to restore the property to a safe, accessible condition.

End-of-Tenancy Clearance

Fast, thorough clearance of rental properties between tenancies or following abandoned tenancies. We remove left-behind contents, clean and prepare the property so landlords and agents can re-list quickly.

Chemical & Drug Remediation

Specialised deep cleaning for properties affected by chemical contamination or drug activity. Rooms and surfaces are professionally cleaned to remove contaminants and prepare the property for safe re-entry or sale.

Use the quote form on our website or call 1300 264 318. We can quote remotely from photos — so you don't need to be at the property to get started. Most quotes are returned within 24 hours.

Can you manage the clearance if I'm not in Melbourne?

Yes. We regularly assist families managing Ivanhoe estate clearances from interstate or overseas. We quote from photos, provide progress updates during the job, and send a complete digital report on completion.

How much does estate clearance cost in Ivanhoe?

Costs vary depending on property size, volume of contents and services required. We provide a fixed written quote before any work begins — so there are no surprises. Submit a quote request with photos for the most accurate estimate.

Pay-at-settlement may be available for eligible estate clearances, allowing costs to be paid from property proceeds at settlement. Contact us to discuss your specific situation.
