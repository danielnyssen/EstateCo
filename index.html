Our Services

Estate clearance services

Estate Cleanouts

Full property clear-outs following a passing, handled with care and discretion.

Families and executors don’t need to manage the physical work — we take care of everything from sorting and removal through to final clean.

Downsizing & Aged Care Transitions

Whole-home sorting and clearance when moving to a smaller home or aged care facility.

We help separate what to keep, donate, store or remove so the transition is calm, organised and manageable.

Hoarded & Neglected Properties

Clearance of heavily cluttered or long-neglected homes requiring more than standard cleaning.

Our team works discreetly and without judgement to restore the property to a safe, accessible condition.

Chemical & Drug Remediation

Specialised deep cleaning for properties affected by chemical contamination or drug activity.

Rooms and surfaces are professionally cleaned to remove contaminants and prepare the property for safe re-entry or sale.

Pre-Sale Property Preparation

Clearing unwanted contents and preparing homes for real estate photography, inspection or settlement.

The property is presented clean, open and ready for market without owners needing to lift a finger.

End-of-Tenancy & Rental Clearance

Fast, thorough clearance of rental properties between tenancies or following abandoned tenancies.

We remove left-behind contents, clean and prepare the property so landlords and agents can re-list quickly and without hassle.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can't find the answer? Call 1300264318.

+

How much does estate clearance cost in Australia?

Costs can vary depending on the size of the property, amount of contents, access, disposal needs and whether cleaning or preparation work is also required. Photos and property details can help give a clearer indication before work is arranged.

+

Is estate clearance tax deductible?

Tax treatment can depend on the circumstances of the estate or property, so it is best to seek advice from an accountant or qualified adviser. Where needed, invoices and job details can help families or executors keep clear records for their own administration.

+

How quickly can you start? What's the timeline?

We provide a quote promptly of receiving your photos or video.

+

What happens to the contents of the property?

Before we begin, we identify and set aside any items the family wants to keep.

+

What is the digital estate report?

Once the work is complete, we provide a digital report showing photos of the property and the completed clearance and cleaning. This gives families, executors and property managers a clear record of the work carried out, particularly when they cannot be present at the property.

+

Do I need to be present at the property?

Not necessarily. In many cases the family, executor or owner is unable to attend the property due to location, timing or other responsibilities. Where appropriate, access arrangements can be coordinated and progress updates provided so the work can continue without requiring someone to be onsite the entire time. This helps reduce the practical burden during what can already be a difficult period.

+

How does the remote quote process work?

You can usually start by sending through property details and photos or a short video using the quote form. This helps provide an initial understanding of the property, access and likely scope of work before any next steps are discussed.

+

What is probate and do I need it before clearance can begin?

Probate requirements can depend on the estate, the property and who has authority to make decisions. It is best to confirm the legal position with the executor, solicitor or relevant representative before arranging a full clearance, particularly where ownership or access has not yet been confirmed.

Our story

A Melbourne family business built to make a difficult process easier

Estate Co. is a Melbourne-based family business created after seeing how difficult it can be for families to manage a home during times of transition. Whether after the passing of a loved one, a move into aged care, or when a property has simply become overwhelming, the process of clearing and preparing a home can feel stressful, time-consuming and emotionally heavy.

We believed there should be a better way — one that is organised, respectful and genuinely supportive. Our team helps families, executors, property owners and real estate agents clear, clean and prepare homes with care, discretion and professionalism so they don’t have to face the process alone.

Our goal is simple — to help people when they need it most. Every home is handled with care, compassion and respect for the people and memories connected to it.
