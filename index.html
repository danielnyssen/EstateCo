Common questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can't find the answer? Call 0401 857 730.

+

How much does deceased estate clearance cost in Australia?

Most deceased estate clearances range from $1,200 for a small studio apartment to $15,000+ for a large acreage property. We provide a fixed, all-inclusive quote promptly of receiving your photos or video — no hourly rates, no surprises.

+

Is deceased estate clearance tax deductible?

Deceased estate clearance costs are generally a legitimate estate expense, deductible against the estate — not against the personal income of beneficiaries or executors. We provide full itemised invoices suitable for estate accounting. We recommend confirming the tax treatment with your estate solicitor or accountant, as each estate is different.

+

How quickly can you start? What's the timeline?

We provide a quote promptly of receiving your photos or video. For urgent situations, we can have someone on-site within 48 hours of request and commence work within 5 business days of quote acceptance. These timelines are publicly stated commitments — not estimates. If you have an urgent situation, call us directly on 0401 857 730.

+

What happens to the contents of the property?

Before we begin, we identify and set aside any items the family wants to keep. Remaining contents are sorted into: (1) items to sell — auctioned or sold on behalf of the estate; (2) items to donate — given to charities with donation receipts; (3) items to dispose of — responsibly recycled or removed. Every item is accounted for in your digital estate report.

+

What is the digital estate report?

At completion, every client receives a comprehensive itemised PDF report detailing everything removed from the property — what was sold (with sale amounts), what was donated (to which charities), and what was disposed of. This document is critical for executor legal obligations and beneficiary transparency, and satisfies probate court requirements.

+

Do I need to be present at the property?

No — our service is designed to be fully remote. We quote via photo or video upload, conduct all consultations via phone or Zoom, and deliver all documentation digitally. Many of our clients are interstate or overseas — in the UK, USA, and Singapore. You never need to book a flight or travel to the property.

+

How does the remote quote process work?

Upload photos or a short video of the property using the form on this page. Our team reviews and sends a fixed quote to your email promptly. You can then book a Zoom or phone consultation to discuss, ask questions, and approve the quote. Everything — including the service agreement — is handled digitally.

+

What is probate and do I need it before clearance can begin?

Probate is the legal process that confirms a will's validity and authorises the executor to deal with the estate. In most Australian states, you do not need to wait for probate to be granted before beginning clearance — particularly if the executor has identified and set aside disputed items. We work with your solicitor to understand what can proceed. Our estate valuation service assists with the probate application itself.
