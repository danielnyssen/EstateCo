Questions

Estate clearance Brisbane FAQs

Do you handle deceased estate clearances in Brisbane?

Yes. We support deceased estate cleanouts across Brisbane, including clearing furniture, sorting household items, coordinating disposal and preparing the property for sale or handover.

Can you help if I live interstate or cannot attend?

Yes. We can work remotely using photos, videos, phone calls and access instructions. This is especially useful for executors, beneficiaries and family members who are not based near the property.

Can you prepare the property for sale?

Yes. Depending on the property, we can coordinate clearance, cleaning, garden tidy-up and presentation preparation so the home is easier to inspect, photograph or hand over to an agent.
